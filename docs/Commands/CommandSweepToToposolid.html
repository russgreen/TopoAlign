Sweep to Toposolid

Converts a road made with Sweep Along Alignment into a toposolid. The toposolid's boundary is the outline of the road in plan and its top surface follows the top of the road, including crown, kerbs and falls. This is Revit 2024 and later only.

Usage / Steps

  1. Select a road first, or launch Sweep to Toposolid and click Pick road. Roads made as a direct shape or as a family are both accepted.
  2. Choose the Toposolid type and the Level the toposolid is placed on. The level starts as the highest one at or below the bottom of the road.
  3. Tick Delete the original road if you no longer want the swept road, then click Create Toposolid.

Demo Video

Options / Parameters

  • Toposolid type — The type sets the thickness and layers of the toposolid. The toposolid hangs below the surface of the road. The last choice is remembered.
  • Level — The level the toposolid is hosted on.
  • Delete the original road — Removes the swept road after the toposolid has been made (off by default). A road made as a family is deleted along with its family.

Notes

  • Only the upward-facing surface of the road is used. The underside and sides of the road are not reproduced; the thickness comes from the toposolid type.
  • The toposolid is a snapshot: it does not update when the alignment or the road changes. Run Sweep Along Alignment again and convert the new road to replace it.
  • If the road has gaps (spans Revit could not build), the toposolid gets a separate boundary for each piece, and the summary says so.
  • If Revit will not make the toposolid with its points in one step, the boundary is made first and the points are added to it afterwards, and the summary says so.
  • A long road gives a toposolid with many points. Use a larger section spacing in Sweep Along Alignment if the result is slow to work with.
